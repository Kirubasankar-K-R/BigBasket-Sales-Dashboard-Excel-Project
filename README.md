# BigBasket-Sales-Dashboard

## 🛒 BigBasket Sales Dashboard | Excel Project

### 1. Project Overview

The BigBasket Sales Dashboard is an interactive Excel-based analytics project designed to analyze retail sales performance, customer preferences, outlet performance, and product category trends across different outlet locations and sizes.

This dashboard transforms raw retail data into meaningful business insights using dynamic charts, KPIs, slicers, and advanced Excel visualization techniques.

---

## 2. Project Objective

The main objectives of this project are:

* To analyze overall retail sales performance
* To monitor product category sales trends
* To identify top-performing outlets and products
* To track sales distribution across outlet locations and sizes
* To visualize customer rating and item visibility trends
* To create an interactive business dashboard using Excel

## 7. Key Insights Generated

Important business insights identified from the dashboard include:

* Supermarket outlets generate higher sales revenue
* Low-fat products show strong customer demand
* Tier 3 locations contribute significantly to sales
* Medium-sized outlets perform better in terms of revenue
* Certain product categories consistently achieve high sales
* Customer ratings help identify popular products
